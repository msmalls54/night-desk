## Research behind “Why this matters now”

- **51%:** US Gen Z respondents who currently owned or had previously owned cryptocurrency in Gemini exchange's 2024 survey. The article was published January 25, 2025. [Original Gemini exchange research](https://www.gemini.com/blog/gemini-survey-finds-more-than-half-of-gen-z-owns-crypto%20).
- **34%:** year-over-year increase in Gen Z betting participation reported for Q2 2025. [Original TransUnion release](https://newsroom.transunion.com/gen-z-millennial-speculators-drove-year-over-year-gambling-growth-in-q2-2025/).

The figures describe separate studies and measures. Gemini exchange's survey is a research source; Google's Gemini is the model used by Night Desk.
